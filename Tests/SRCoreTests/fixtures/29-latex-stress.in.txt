\section{Stress Test}
Consider $\sqrt[3]{x}$ and $\left(\frac{a+b}{c}\right)^{2}$ with $\mathbb{R}$ and $\mathcal{O}(n \log n)$.
The sum $\sum_{i=1}^{N} x_i$ and the limit $\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sin x}{x} = 1$ both appear.
\begin{gather}
a = b \\
c = d^{\dagger}
\end{gather}
We write $\vec{F} = m\ddot{x}$ and $\hat{H}\psi = E\psi$, plus $x \cdots y$ and $A^{T}$.
The integral $\int_0^\infty e^{-x}\,dx$ converges\footnote{By dominated convergence.}.
